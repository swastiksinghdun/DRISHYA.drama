About Drishya



"We must all do theatre, to find out who we are, and what we can become."
Augusto Boal



For centuries, theatre has been a powerful medium for storytelling, self-expression and social commentary. We at Drishya aim to capture the essence of this art and bring it to the students of IIT Bhilai.

We perform acts, plays and skits that make events memorable and entertaining. Our team of passionate performers and creative minds work tirelessly to create engaging and thought provoking.

Independence Day to Fresher's Night, we have been a part of various events and have left our mark on the stage. We also represent IIT Bhilai at Inter IIT Cultural Festivals, showcasing the talent of our institute to the wider audience.

If you ever wanted a platform to showcase your alter egos or are just curious about play, come join the stage.
